About this model. TF-IDF word, word-pair and letter-chunk features with class-balanced logistic regression (following Davidson et al., 2017). It was trained on 24,783 labelled tweets plus everyday tweets from OLID (Zampieri et al., 2019), with negation marking and identity-word swaps.

Limits. The tweets date from 2017 and are English only. The policy results assume human reviewers always make the right call. "AAE-aligned" is estimated from language by a dialect model (Blodgett et al., 2016), not from who wrote the post. This page explains one research model; it is not a tool for checking real posts.

Built from github.com/SanjanaJairam/abusive-language-detection, where the notebooks show every step in code.

Example sentences in the style of HateCheck (Röttger et al., 2021) and the identity templates of Dixon et al. (2018).
